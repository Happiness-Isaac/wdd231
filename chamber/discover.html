About the Area

  • Lekki is a city in Lagos State, Nigeria, to the southeast of Lagos city proper. It is a naturally formed peninsula, adjoining Victoria Island and Ikoyi to the west, with the Atlantic Ocean to its south, Lagos Lagoon to the north, and Lekki Lagoon to its east.
  • The peninsula is approximately 70 to 80 km long, with an average width of 10 km.
  • Lekki is known for its luxury real estate, bustling commercial hubs, beaches, and wildlife conservation areas.
  • Population: the area falls mainly within Eti-Osa Local Government Area (287,785 residents at the 2006 census) and Ibeju-Lekki Local Government Area (117,481 residents at the 2006 census).
  • The proposed land-use master plan for Lekki envisages the peninsula as a "Blue-Green Environment City", expected to accommodate a residential population of over 3.4 million in addition to a non-residential population of at least 1.9 million.
